A snoot is a tube that clamps onto the front of the flash and funnels the
light into a narrow cone. A honeycomb grid can be added inside or over the
front opening to further tighten the beam. The MagSnoot used here extends in
four stages — each stage narrows the beam and costs roughly one stop of
output — and can optionally take a grid at every stage.
