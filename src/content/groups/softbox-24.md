A 24-inch square softbox mounted on a speedring. The light enters through a
back opening, bounces around inside the silvered interior, and exits through
one or more diffusion layers at the front. An optional honeycomb grid clips
over the front face to narrow the spill.

Four configurations are shown: bare (no diffusion at all), inner baffle only,
outer diffusion only, and outer diffusion with the grid.
